### 💼 Featured Production Projects

#### 1. 🚗 Acas Variasi — Automotive Operations, HRMS & Client Portal Ecosystem
> *Full-stack ecosystem integrating a public booking/recruitment portal with an enterprise workshop operations back-office.*
- **Public Client & Booking Portal (`acas-laravel`):**
  - **Dynamic Multi-Subdomain:** Dynamically routes between `recruitment.acasvariasi.com` (candidate job portal, 1-click apply, digital CV PDF generator) and `booking.acasvariasi.com` (automotive modification & audio booking).
  - **Real-Time Candidate Hub:** In-app notification center with WITA (UTC+8) localization, unread status indicators, and instant AJAX updates.
- **Enterprise Operations Dashboard (`acas-variasi-dashboard`):**
  - **Workshop SOP & Inspection:** Digital inspection checklists before and after installation, continuous DB state persistence (`db-start`, `db-save`, `db-finish`), photo evidence capture, supervisor verification, and official PDF inspection sheet generation (DomPDF).
  - **Calendar Service Scheduling:** Interactive reservation calendar per branch to prevent vehicle service queue conflicts.
  - **Full HRMS & Payroll Suite:** Staff attendance tracking, overtime work orders (`/perintah-lembur`), multi-level leave approvals, monthly Excel audit recaps, and automated salary slip generation.
- **Tech:** `Laravel 11/12`, `PHP 8.2+`, `TailwindCSS 3.x`, `MySQL 8.0+`, `Vite`, `DomPDF`, `Maatwebsite Excel`, `Pest PHP`.

#### 2. 📊 TDA — Enterprise Financial & Cash Flow Management System
- Multi-role financial dashboard (Webmaster, Finance, Divisions) with strict data isolation.
- Built a dynamic Cash Flow Report generator processing raw accounting journal data (Debit/Credit) into granular business reports.
- Automated QRIS and bank transfer reconciliation against general ledgers (`lap_jurnal`), eliminating manual errors.
- End-to-end fund request workflow (*Pencairan Dana Kegiatan*) with approval hierarchies and programmatic CSV/Excel/PDF exports.
- **Tech:** `Laravel`, `JavaScript`, `TailwindCSS`, `MySQL`, `RESTful APIs`.

#### 3. 🌲 Petapohon — Real-Time Urban GIS & Tree Monitoring Platform
- Real-time municipal tree monitoring platform integrating GPS field data collection and interactive spatial mapping.
- Role-based workflow system for field validation and administrative utility task dispatching.
- **Tech:** `GIS Integration`, `Flutter`, `Laravel`, `MySQL`, `RESTful APIs`.

#### 4. 🛒 Booble & Sewaja ERM — Multi-Platform POS & Accounting Systems
- Maintained and optimized multi-platform cashier (POS), inventory control, and financial accounting modules (Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow).
- Overhauled legacy financial structures, reinforced database integrity constraints, and audited data encryption across transactional layers.
- **Tech:** `PHP`, `Laravel`, `CodeIgniter`, `MySQL`, `Security Hardening`.

#### 5. 🥽 Festival Tenun Tradisional Bugis — 360° Virtual Cultural Tour
- Engineered an interactive, immersive 360° virtual cultural exhibition website with WebGL and VR-ready components.
- **Tech:** `WebGL`, `JavaScript`, `VR Components`, `CSS3`.
